# The first-run tutorial

The first time ReVox opens it shows a short tutorial over the Live tab: seven pages that walk through what the app does, each with a small working demo rather than a paragraph. Welcome says what ReVox is (it hears, translates on the phone, speaks, keeps a transcript — nothing leaves the iPhone). Choose a source is the Live screen's Microphone / Other apps control, to tap. Start and read is a miniature transcript: tap Start and a scripted conversation arrives one phrase at a time, with the language badge, the English translation and the age counting up, until you tap Stop. Two-way turns the toggle on and shows the Spanish reply appear under the other person's line, with the "Don't translate / Reply in" pair explained. Learning switches the original line on above the translation, then romanization under it. Models and voices lets you tap through the five Whisper models and reads what each costs, says that voices are optional, and shows what Keep my model when hot does. Ready recaps the three steps; Start translating closes the tutorial. Skip is in the top corner throughout, Back and Next at the bottom, and a swipe turns the page. Under Reduce Motion the pages crossfade and nothing bounces; every page is one VoiceOver unit with a heading.

To see it again, go to **Settings** and tap **Show the tutorial** in the last group, under About. That forgets the completion and presents the tutorial from the first page with the demos reset.

Completion is one integer in `UserDefaults.standard`, key `onboarding_completed_version`: the version of the tutorial that was finished or skipped. The constant is `OnboardingViewModel.version` in `ReVoxMobile/Screens/Onboarding/OnboardingViewModel.swift`. Bump it when the tutorial changes enough that someone who finished the old one should see the new one; a stored version lower than the constant shows the tutorial at the next launch, a higher one (a downgrade) does not. The pages and their copy live in `OnboardingPage.swift`, the screens in `OnboardingView.swift`, and `OnboardingTests`, `OnboardingHostingTests` and `OnboardingScreenshotTests` cover the model, every page in both demo states, and the `onboarding-welcome.png` / `onboarding-transcript.png` screenshots CI collects with the others.
