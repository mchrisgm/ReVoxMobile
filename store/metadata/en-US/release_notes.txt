The first App Store release of ReVox.

Live translation of speech into English, entirely on the iPhone: from the microphone, or from other apps through a screen broadcast. Nothing leaves the phone, and after the one-time model download the app works fully offline.

Two-way conversation: what they say is spoken to you in English, and what you say is spoken to them in their language (iOS 18).

Learning mode: the words as spoken above each translation, and a tap on any word for how it sounds, what it means and the sentence it came from.

Five model sizes, and Benchmark this iPhone to see which one keeps up on your phone.

Three downloadable pocket-tts voices, or the iPhone's own voice with no download. Other apps are turned down while ReVox speaks.

History with search, merge and .txt export, stored only on the phone.

A seven-page tutorial on the first launch, and an About screen with every licence.
